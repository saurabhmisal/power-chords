WORKFLOW DIAGRAM

How Beacon works.

Follow the numbered sequence. If evidence or access is missing, resolve it before advancing. Review the result before taking an external action.

  1. INPUT

    Account notes · stakeholder list · CRM exports · meeting transcripts

  2. STEP 01

    Identify the account's objectives and constraints

  3. STEP 02

    Map roles and decision influence

  4. STEP 03

    Summarize commitments with owners

  5. STEP 04

    Mark unanswered questions and access boundaries

  6. STEP 05

    Prepare targeted follow-up questions

  7. REVIEW

    Avoid speculating about private motives or personal attributes

  8. OUTPUT

    Account brief · stakeholder map · open-items ledger

Approval gate → Review the deliverables. If changes are needed, return to the relevant step. Publish, send, deploy or change external systems only after explicit approval.
