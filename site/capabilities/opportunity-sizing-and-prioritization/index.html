WORKFLOW DIAGRAM

How Summit works.

Follow the numbered sequence. If evidence or access is missing, resolve it before advancing. Review the result before taking an external action.

  1. INPUT

    Opportunity list · customer impact signals · implementation estimates

  2. STEP 01

    Choose scoring criteria and weights

  3. STEP 02

    Capture supporting evidence

  4. STEP 03

    Evaluate impact, confidence, effort and risk

  5. STEP 04

    Show sensitivity to uncertain scores

  6. STEP 05

    Recommend a decision and next validation step

  7. REVIEW

    Use explicitly labeled assumptions rather than fake numerical certainty

  8. OUTPUT

    Priority matrix · decision rationale · assumption list

Approval gate → Review the deliverables. If changes are needed, return to the relevant step. Publish, send, deploy or change external systems only after explicit approval.
